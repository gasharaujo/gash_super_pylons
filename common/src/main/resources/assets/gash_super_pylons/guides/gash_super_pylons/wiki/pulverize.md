# Crush Upgrade

Crushes blocks and ores, including compatible recipes added by a datapack. Costs 1,000 FE per item or furnace fuel. Quantity changes supported yields and drop chances.

With SCC installed on NeoForge 1.21.1 or 26.1.2, an automatic Processing Calculator facing the pylon shows the Crush upgrade and its Quantity tier. The terminal uses the active tier’s recipes. Aim the pylon’s working side at the Calculator to export the results to the SCC network.
