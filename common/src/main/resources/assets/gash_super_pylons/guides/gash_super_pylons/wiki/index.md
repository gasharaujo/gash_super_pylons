# Gash's Super Pylons

Craft a Super Pylon with eight iron ingots around chiseled stone bricks. In Creative, the mod has its own tab with the pylon icon.

<GameScene zoom="3">
  <Block id="gash_super_pylons:super_pylon" y="1" />
  <Block id="minecraft:chest" />
</GameScene>

Right-click to deposit items. A basic pylon holds up to 2,048 items of one exact kind, including matching item data. Hit the pylon to withdraw one, or sneak and hit to withdraw a stack. In Creative, a pickaxe breaks the pylon.

Right-click with an upgrade to install it. Sneak and right-click with an empty main hand to remove the rightmost icon stamped on the frame. The first upgrade out of a generator and processor pair is the generator.
