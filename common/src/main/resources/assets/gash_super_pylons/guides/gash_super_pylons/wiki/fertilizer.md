# Fertilizer Upgrade

Advances nearby plant growth using random ticks. Super advances plant stages directly.

Crimson and warped fungi grow on their matching nylium. With AgriCraft installed, Fertilizer and Super use AgriCraft’s bone meal rules for planted crops and skip empty crop sticks.
