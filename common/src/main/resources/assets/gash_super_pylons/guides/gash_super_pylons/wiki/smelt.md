# Smelt Upgrade

Processes furnace recipes for 1,000 FE per item, with furnace fuel as a fallback. It accepts a smoker or blast furnace attachment. Super processes the inventory opposite the output.

With SCC installed on NeoForge 1.21.1 or 26.1.2, an automatic Processing Calculator facing the pylon shows the Smelt upgrade and detects its furnace mode. Aim the pylon’s working side at the Calculator to export the results to the SCC network.
