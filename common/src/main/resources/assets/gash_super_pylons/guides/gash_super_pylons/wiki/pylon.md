# Super Pylon

A Super Pylon stores **2,048 items of one exact kind**. Two stacks match only when the item and its data match, including enchantments and custom names. The core shows the stored item. The frame resists explosions and has the mining properties of an iron block.

<RecipeFor id="gash_super_pylons:super_pylon" />

- **Deposit:** right-click with the item in hand.
- **Withdraw:** hit the pylon for one item. Sneak and hit for one stack.
- **Creative:** hit with a pickaxe to break the block. The mod's Creative tab uses the pylon as its icon.

Hoppers and compatible item cables can access the stock. [Upgrades](upgrades.md) add storage, generation or new ways to use the pylon.
