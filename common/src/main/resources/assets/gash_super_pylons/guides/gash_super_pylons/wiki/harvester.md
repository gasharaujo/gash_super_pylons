# Harvester Upgrade

Harvests mature crops, whole trees and giant Nether fungi. Use the Wrench to face an adjacent inventory. The Harvester sends drops there and holds items until space is available. With HT’s TreeChop installed, it can harvest trees with partially chopped logs.

With AgriCraft installed, the Harvester collects mature produce and leaves the crop sticks and plant genetics in place for regrowth. With SCC installed on NeoForge 1.21.1 or 26.1.2, it can send the harvest to a Processing Calculator on the working side that faces the pylon and is connected to the network. These integrations are optional.

With Super it attacks nearby mobs. Equip a weapon by sneaking and right-clicking the pylon with it.
