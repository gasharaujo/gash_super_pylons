# Enchanter

## Enchant an item

1. Install **Enchanter** on the main pylon. Use the [binding walkthrough](workshops.md) to link one XP Tank and one item auxiliary. In **Bind** mode, sneak and right-click the main pylon, then release Sneak and right-click both auxiliaries.
2. Put **lapis lazuli** in the item auxiliary. Right-click the main pylon with an unenchanted, enchantable item or a plain book.
3. Each successful operation consumes **one lapis and 27 XP points** from the auxiliaries. The enchanted item or book goes to the working-side chest. If it stays in the core, hit the main pylon to take it out.

## Tune the enchantments

Install the modifiers on the **main pylon**. Speed shortens the time between attempts. Quantity sets the minimum number of compatible enchantments the roll tries to include. The Enchanter uses the available compatible enchantments when it cannot reach that minimum.

The base enchanting power is **1**. Install **Super** on the main pylon to let Capacity raise that power.

| Tier | None | I | II | III | IV | V | VI |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Power with Super and Capacity | 1 | 10 | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 | 100 |
| Minimum with Quantity | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |

With Apotheosis or Apothic Enchanting installed, right-click the main pylon with a supported shelf to add its effects. The Enchanter holds up to 64 shelves.

## Exclude enchantments with Super

Install **Super** on the Enchanter. Link the XP Tank and lapis auxiliary first. Select the main pylon again with the Wrench in Bind mode and right-click a **third** pylon within the same distance limit. Put an enchanted item or book in its core. All enchantments on that sample are excluded from future rolls. The sample stays in place and the third pylon keeps its normal core display.
